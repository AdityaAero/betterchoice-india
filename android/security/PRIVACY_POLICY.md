## What the app does

BetterChoice displays a small bubble when a supported shopping app is active and
removes it when you leave. Version 1 does not analyse food or recommend products.

## Accessibility access

With your explicit consent and Android accessibility permission, BetterChoice
accesses window information and the root package name to determine which app is
active. It processes app transitions globally so it can hide the bubble when you
leave a supported app. Unsupported package names are discarded immediately.
Android grants broader window-content access for this capability, but BetterChoice
does not traverse the screen's view tree, read screen text or images, capture
screenshots, monitor keystrokes, or perform actions in other apps.

The app is not an accessibility tool intended primarily for people with disabilities.
It respects Android restrictions and does not circumvent protected screen content.
App detection does not identify individual checkout or payment pages.

## Local storage and sharing

BetterChoice stores only your accepted disclosure version and the bubble's screen
position in the app's private storage. It keeps no browsing or shopping history,
retailer identifiers, product information, account identifiers, or location.
It checks whether Android Advanced Protection restricts the companion; this state
is not stored. Backup and device transfer of app data are excluded.

Version 1 has no Internet permission, accounts, advertising, analytics, or remote
crash reporting. It sends no app data to the publisher or third parties. Platform
services such as Google Play and Android diagnostics operate under their own policies.
Developer debug builds have fixed diagnostic messages and timing measurements;
the distributed release build has no BetterChoice runtime logging.

## Your choices

Accessibility permission is optional; without it, the bubble cannot operate.
Use **Turn off BetterChoice** to withdraw consent and stop the companion, or disable
**BetterChoice bubble** in Android Accessibility settings. Clearing app storage or
uninstalling removes the saved consent and position. Reinstallation requires setup.

If a future version changes data access or processing, its disclosure and policy
will be updated and new consent obtained before that processing begins.

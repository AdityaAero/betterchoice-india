# Viability: 1,000+ products read, rated and explained

For the founder and the certified nutritionist. 1268 real Indian packaged foods and drinks (Open Food Facts India, labels from 2025, ODbL) were written into a real recorded Zepto product page, in the two ways Zepto prints nutrition, and read by the app's own pipeline exactly as the bubble reads a screen. Six shoppers were then shown the card: no condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, heart or cholesterol, weight loss and muscle gain. Zepto itself was not browsed at this scale (bulk automated access to its app); products are the ones Indian shoppers buy, by brand and category.

## Reading

- Zepto's usual format ("Energy (kcal) 345.0, Protein (g) 52.0, ..."): **1264 of 1268 rated** (100%); the colour matches the label's true figures for **1250** (99% of those rated).
- Zepto's bracket format with no energy ("Protein(52.5g), Carbohydrate(31.5g), ..."; ZOFF Soya Chunks was listed this way): **1242 of 1264 rated**, colour matches for **1231** (99%). Energy is worked out from protein, carbohydrate and fat by FSSAI's factors, and the card says so.
- Where the colour differed (14): Amul Amul Tru Sitaphal Bliss (label C, read E); Alpino High Protein Muesli Cold Coffee (label E, read C); Complan Power Play (label B, read C); Regency Regency Protein Trail Mix (label A, read D); Hanobar mango creme milk (label D, read B); PepsiCo Inc. Sting energy (label E, read B); Paper boat Paper boat NaTa mixed berries (label E, read C); Real, dabur Green Apple (label E, read C); Hershey's HERSHEY'S (label E, read C); Sting Sting Energy (label E, read B); Paper Boat paper boat orange pch (label E, read C); Farmley Trail Mix (label C, read D).

## How often is it red?

Across all 1268: green 305 (24%), yellow 204 (16%), red 759 (60%). Of the red, Nutri-Score D 261 and E 498: on Nutri-Score's own five-colour scale D is orange and only E is red, so showing the official scale would turn 21% of products from red to orange.

Red products still show what is good about them: **641 of 749** red products (86%) get at least one dark-green line (protein, fibre, low sugar, low sodium, unsaturated fat) for a shopper with no condition.

## What each shopper sees

Dark red on the card is what counts against the product; the shopper's own reasons come first, tagged ("your BP", "your diabetes", "your heart"), and a condition makes its nutrient count from where a food stops being "low" in it (FSSAI's claim levels: 120 mg sodium, 5 g sugar, 1.5 g saturated fat per 100 g). Dark green is what is good about it.

|---|---|---|---|
| No condition | - | 463 of 666 (70%) | 116 |
| High blood pressure | 670 (53%) | 404 of 666 (61%) | 160 |
